A current forecast to support the FBI’s small business mission

The FBI’s Small Business Program helps ensure small businesses receive consideration in the acquisitions that support the Bureau’s mission. This concept is designed to reduce the effort of collecting, checking, and consolidating forecast updates, giving the SBP more time to work with acquisition teams and small businesses.

Designed to run on Power Apps Power Automate SharePoint Outlook

Select a topic to learn more

  • Less time chasing updates

    Targeted requests and response tracking reduce manual follow-up with requirement owners.

  • More current information for acquisition discussions

    Consistent descriptions, dates, and acquisition approaches give a stronger starting point with requirement owners and contracting staff.

  • Better information for small business outreach

    Accurate NAICS codes, values, and dates help small businesses see where they fit and prepare in time.

  • Review effort focused where judgment matters

    Routine checks flag missing details and formatting issues, so review time goes to changes that need a decision.

  • A simpler submission

    Approved entries export directly to DOJ’s format, with no consolidation or reformatting.

  1. Requirement owner

    Enters or updates an entry

  2. Small Business POC

    Gets an email right away

    Key details and open items, one click from the submission.

  3. Small Business POC

    Reviews and approves

    Changes shown side by side. Approve, adjust, or send back with a note.

  4. Small Business POC

    Exports in DOJ’s format

    DOJ’s 34-column layout, ready to submit with no reformatting.

The concept is designed for tools that are part of Microsoft 365.

  • Data stays in the FBI’s environment

    Entries would live in a SharePoint list in the FBI’s own Microsoft 365 tenant. Nothing moves to an outside system.

  • Familiar Microsoft tools

    The app would be built in Power Apps, part of Microsoft 365, rather than as separate software.

  • Notifications in familiar tools

    Power Automate would send update requests and alerts through Outlook. It’s designed around standard connectors, so it shouldn’t need premium licenses.
